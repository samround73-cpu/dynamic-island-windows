# What's new

Newest first. Updates install themselves (Settings › About › Updates), so you don't need to download these by hand.

## 0.44
- **Brightness on the Controls tab:** a little toggle next to the big slider switches it between volume and brightness. It only appears on screens whose brightness can be changed (laptops, and most monitors), so not on Shadow.
- **Fade when idle** (Settings › Behaviour): after 10 s, 30 s, 1 min or 5 min without the mouse nearby, the island fades to a faint ghost, and comes straight back when the mouse heads up there or something pops out.
- **See-through Light style** (Settings › Appearance): a slider from fully solid down to 50%.

## 0.43
- **More than one screen:** the island moves to whichever screen your mouse rests on. It tucks up into the top of the old screen and drops into the new one. Prefer it to stay put? Settings › Behaviour › Main screen.
- **New song pop-out:** the app, the song, play/pause and skip buttons, and how far through it is. It waits while your mouse is on it. Turn it off in Settings › Pop-ups.
- **Pop-outs stay up for** Short, Normal or Long (Settings › Pop-ups).
- **Clock settings** (new Settings › Clock): 12 or 24-hour or follow Windows, seconds, and how the date shows on the pill, or hide it.
- **Shelf & clipboard settings:** clean things off the Shelf after 1, 7 or 30 days (the files stay put), and clear clipboard history when you lock the PC.

## 0.42
- **Sound page on the Controls tab** (the arrow on the Sound row): pick where sound plays (speakers, headphones, TV), mute everything with the switch, and give every app its own volume. Apps playing right now are listed first with a gold slider; click an app's speaker to mute just that app.

## 0.41
- **Pin folders, files, websites and Settings pages** to the hover tray, next to your apps. Use the **+** at the end of the row, or drag something onto the pill and choose **Pin to tray** or **Put on Shelf**. Hover a pinned folder to peek at its newest files; a pinned Downloads folder gets a dot when something new arrives.
- **Shelf actions:** tick several files to copy, zip or show them together; right-click for Open with, Edit, Copy image, Rename, Save a copy, Pin to tray, Delete and more. The screenshot pop-out has quick Copy, Edit and Delete buttons.
- **Background app icons** (OneDrive, Discord, Steam…) appear as named chips on the Controls tab, with their own menus. Can be turned off in Settings › Behaviour.
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth lists** on the Controls tab (the arrow on each row): connect to networks, and connect or disconnect headphones and speakers.
- **0.41.5:** the screenshot pop-out stays for 7 seconds, and stays put while your mouse is on it (hovering it no longer swaps it for the tray), so there's time to press Copy, Edit or Delete.
- **0.41.4:** snips are now picked up on cloud and remote PCs (like Shadow), where Windows hands the copied picture over through a background service. Copying the same snip again doesn't add it twice, and drawing on a snip in Snipping Tool updates the saved file.
- **0.41.3:** snips taken with Win+Shift+S now land on the Shelf even when Snipping Tool isn't set to save them automatically (the island saves the copied snip to Pictures › Screenshots itself).

## 0.40
- **Light and dark switch instantly.** Changing style, or Windows switching between light and dark with **Auto**, no longer restarts the island.
- **Light keeps the island's shape**, with crisper white cards on a soft light-grey island for better contrast.
- **Frosted blur** is now an optional switch for the Light style (Windows can only blur a plain rounded panel, so the curved shoulders go when it's on).

## 0.39
- **New Light style** replaces Glass: dark text on a light island, with the accent colour deepened so it stays readable.
- **Auto** style: Black or Light, following Windows' own light/dark mode.

## 0.38
- **Tidier pill:** time and date, then battery, music bars or download speed after small dots, all centred. The pill grows to fit.
- **Tidier hover tray:** the song and any download share one card, and every open app sits in one row with a ring around the one you're using.

## 0.37
- **Pin apps to the hover tray** (right-click › Pin to tray). Pinned apps that aren't open show as faded outlines; click to start them.
- **Download progress:** a glow runs along the pill while something downloads, with the speed; hover for details.
- **Caps Lock pop-out** when you turn it on or off.

## 0.33 – 0.36
- **Screenshot and download pop-outs**, with new files put on the Shelf ready to drag somewhere.
- **Windows Settings pages in search** ("bluetooth", "display"…).
- **Show, hide and reorder the tabs** (Settings › Tabs).
- Fixes: no more restarts or hiding while taking a screenshot; updates are more reliable.

## 0.29 – 0.32
- **Ctrl+Space** opens app search from anywhere.
- New **Today** and **Settings** layouts.
- **Starts with Windows** more safely, and reopens when you reconnect (Shadow, Remote Desktop) or unlock.
- **Automatic updates** from this page, and **Remove from this PC…** in Settings › About.
- Ready-made **DynamicIsland.exe** downloads.
